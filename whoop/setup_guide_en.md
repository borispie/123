# WHOOP Setup Guide (English)

> Steps follow the current official app. Update screenshots if the UI changes.

1. **Charge**: Fully charge the battery pack, then clip it onto the band.
2. **Get the app**: Search "WHOOP" on the App Store / Google Play.
3. **Sign in** with the email you used for your membership.
4. **Pair**: Turn on Bluetooth and follow the in-app pairing steps with the band close to your phone.
5. **Profile**: Enter accurate height, weight and birthday — they affect strain and calorie estimates.
6. **Fit**: Wear it about one finger-width above your wrist bone, snug enough that only one finger fits underneath. A loose fit hurts heart-rate accuracy.
